# Accommodations & Job Search

Practical supports for AuDHD adults, including those over 50 navigating employment.

## Workplace Accommodations

Under the ADA (U.S.), ADHD and autism can qualify as disabilities when they substantially limit major life activities. Most accommodations are low- or no-cost.

**Common ideas** (individualized; consult JAN):

- Sensory: noise-canceling headphones, adjustable lighting, quiet workspace, reduced fluorescent exposure
- Communication: written instructions, agendas in advance, clear structured workflows, fewer interviewers
- Executive function / time: visual schedules, body doubling, flexible start times, deadline check-ins, task breakdown support
- Transitions & change: advance notice, predictable routines where possible
- Remote/hybrid options when role allows

**Key resource**: Job Accommodation Network — free consulting. https://askjan.org/  | Phone: 800-526-7234

State vocational rehabilitation agencies can help with job search, training, and assistive tech.

## Job Search & Inclusive Hiring

- ND-specific boards and filters: Mentra, Neurodiversity Career Connector (NDCC), Hire Autism, Specialisterne, Aspiritech, etc.
- LinkedIn / general boards with keywords: "AI support", "neurodiversity", "remote", "senior", "accommodations"
- Company programs (examples): Microsoft Autism Hiring, SAP Autism at Work, JPMorgan, Dell, EY Neurodiversity Centers
- Portfolio approaches that highlight systems thinking, multi-agent orchestration, resilience, and lived ND experience (as used in prior job-search work)

## Older Adults / Mid-Career+ Notes

- Vocational rehab and Ticket to Work remain relevant.
- Combine age-related and ND accommodations (e.g., sensory + reduced context switching).
- Strengths often include pattern recognition, deep focus on passion areas, honesty, systems thinking.

Document functional needs rather than leading with diagnosis if preferred. JAN and state VR can help with the interactive process.
