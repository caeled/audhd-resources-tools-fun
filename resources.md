# Resources

## Core Organizations & Sites

- **Job Accommodation Network (JAN)**: Free, confidential guidance on workplace accommodations and ADA rights. Excellent for ADHD, autism, and neurodiversity. https://askjan.org/ (see Neurodiversity page)
- **ADDitude Magazine**: Articles, webinars, expert Q&As on ADHD.
- **CHADD**: National resource on ADHD.
- **NeuroDiversion**: Tools, games, guides on ADHD/autism/AuDHD. https://www.neurodiversion.org/
- **Neurodivergent Tech Directory** (https://neurodivergenttech.org/): Independent directory of 170–186+ apps and devices matched to how your brain works (ADHD, autism, dyslexia, executive function, etc.). Includes personalized quiz matching, searchable filters, Ask Bob AI assistant for comparisons and budget toolkits, free buyer/assessment guides, and UK Access to Work / workplace resources. Built by ND people for ND people.
- **FeelingsHelper**: Free interactive tools for autism, ADHD, PTSD (masking, executive function, etc.). https://feelingshelper.com/tools
- **The Neurodiversity Hub**: Free downloadable resources (sensory profiles, emotion thermometers, etc.).

## Guides & Workbooks

- AuDHD-specific workbooks (e.g., from AuDHD Feed community): procrastination, sleep, life admin, curiosity/identity.
- Practical guides on routines that balance ADHD novelty needs with autistic preference for predictability.
- Late-diagnosis identity resources and neurodivergent passports.
- Free guides from Neurodivergent Tech Directory: Top 20 Assessment Tools, Complete Buyer Guide to Neurodivergent Technology, Top 20 Neurodivergent Tech Tools of 2026, HR Director’s Guide.

## Research & Context (high-level)

- High co-occurrence of autism and ADHD (often cited 50-70% overlap ranges).
- "Squirrel" as ADHD attention-shift metaphor (from the film *Up*).
- AuDHD as lived experience of competing needs: structure vs. novelty, sensory load vs. stimulation seeking.
