# Fun & Squirrel Stuff

Lightness, memes, symbols, and creative play that affirm AuDHD experience without pathologizing.

## Official AuDHD Squirrel

The squirrel (especially one sitting at or on a computer screen) became a community symbol for:

- Attention being yanked away mid-thought ("Squirrel!")
- Stashing ideas / forgetting then resurfacing awkwardly
- Community shitposting and thread use
- Tattoo designs and job-search artifacts / LinkedIn hooks
- Therapeutic creative projects

Related family squirrel memes (chaotic dad + kids, recovering mom, etc.) and collaborative image ideas with Flynn (e.g., squirrel typing on a giant computer floating in space).

## Creative / Meditative Squirrel Sanctuary

A prompt guide and aesthetic developed for personal therapeutic use:

- Liminal techno-mage style
- Subtle biophilic/tech fusion
- Long-exposure water, atmospheric glows
- Calming, low-pressure creative space for AuDHD regulation

Use for image generation, journaling prompts, or ambient visual rest. Not a replacement for clinical support.

## Other Fun Elements

- ADHD "squirrel brain" mugs, pins, stickers ("Highway to Hey Look a Squirrel", undiagnosed squirrel designs)
- AuDHD creature plushies and community projects
- Daily word games / sensory breaks from sites like NeuroDiversion
- Ambient / dark ambient tracks specifically produced for AuDHD nervous systems (no sudden loud moments, warm low-mids)
- Community spaces: Squirrel Space, various Discord/Threads accounts celebrating neurodivergent squirrel energy

Humor is a valid coping and connection tool. Keep it kind to yourself and others.
